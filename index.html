Search & Retrieval

PaperCompass

Search academic papers by topic, save the ones that matter, then ask questions across everything you've collected.

Keyword search on academic databases turns up plenty of papers that share a word with your topic but have nothing to do with it — so you end up skimming abstracts just to find the handful that matter. And once you've found them, there's no fast way to ask questions across all of them together. PaperCompass fixes both: search results pass through a second AI check that confirms they're genuinely on-topic, and a chat layer lets you query your saved library with answers grounded in the papers themselves, not guesses.

PythonGradioGroq API (gpt-oss-20b)sentence-transformersOpenAlex APIpandas / NumPyHugging Face Spaces
  • Two-stage filtering: keyword search first, then an LLM pass double-checks every result is actually on-topic
  • If a precise search comes up empty, it automatically broadens the search — and tells you it did
  • Multi-turn chat over your saved papers, grounded in what you've actually saved — it won't guess at answers outside that
  • Private, session-only library, with one-click citations in APA, MLA, or IEEE

    Voice-to-Voice Translator

    Speak in one language, hear it back in another — instantly, and at zero API cost.

    Most real-time speech translation tools sit behind paid APIs, which makes them impractical if you just need quick multilingual conversation as a traveler, student, or small business owner. This app runs the entire pipeline — recognizing speech, translating it, speaking it back — locally, with no per-request cost, and it's built to keep working even when a service it depends on gets rate-limited.

    Hiring Tools

    ATS Resume Analyzer

    Upload a resume and a job description, get a transparent match score and a skill-gap breakdown in seconds.

    Most ATS tools match resumes to job descriptions on exact keywords, so someone who wrote "ML" instead of "Machine Learning" can get filtered out even when they're qualified. On the other side, recruiters screening dozens of resumes by hand is slow and inconsistent. This tool matches on keywords and on meaning, and instead of one opaque score, it shows three separate numbers — skills match, keyword density, experience relevance — so you can see why a resume scored the way it did.

    PythonStreamlitGroq API (gpt-oss-20b)sentence-transformersspaCypdfplumber / PyMuPDFTesseract OCR
    • Matches on meaning as well as keywords, so related terms like "predictive analytics" and "Machine Learning" are recognized as connected
    • Score breaks into three parts instead of one black-box number
    • Reads PDF, DOCX, and TXT resumes, including OCR for scanned PDFs
    • Upload several resumes at once and rank them in a sortable, exportable table
    • Flags formatting issues that would trip up a real ATS parser before it becomes a problem
